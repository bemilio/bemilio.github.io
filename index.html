About me

Post-doc

Department of Decision and Control Systems

KTH Stockholm, Sweden

I am a postdoctoral researcher at KTH Royal Institute of Technology, in the Department of Decision and Control Systems, working with Prof. Giuseppe Belgioioso.

My research lies at the intersection of multi-agent model predictive control and game theory, with a focus on stability guarantees, design principles, and numerical methods for game-theoretic controllers.

I completed my Ph.D. in Systems and Control at TU Delft, under the supervision of Prof. Sergio Grammatico, and hold an M.Sc. in Robotics, Systems and Control from ETH Zürich and a B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering from the University of Catania. I have held visiting research positions at UC Santa Barbara, CU Boulder, and the Italian Institute of Technology in Genova.

I have a background in robotics and electrical engineering, which I put to use validating these ideas experimentally on multi-robot coordination, autonomous driving, and power distribution systems.

Research interests

  • Game-theoretic model predictive control
  • Stability and performance guarantees for multi-agent controllers
  • Numerical methods for variational inequalities and Nash equilibrium problems
  • Applications to robotics, autonomous driving, and power systems
